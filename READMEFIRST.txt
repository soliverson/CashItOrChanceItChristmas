HOW TO PLAY

1. Download the ZIP file to your computer.

2. IMPORTANT: Extract the ZIP file before playing.

   Windows:
   Right-click the ZIP → Extract All

   Mac:
   Double-click the ZIP file.

3. Open the extracted game folder.

4. Double-click:
   ▶ START GAME.html

5. Your game will open in your internet browser.

6. For the best experience, put your browser in FULL SCREEN.

That's it — you're ready to play! 🎉

INTERNET IS NOT REQUIRED TO PLAY.
